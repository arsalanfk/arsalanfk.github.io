Interactive · Data visualization

Is the S&P 500 repeating 2000?

A widely shared chart compares today's S&P 500 with the market before the dot-com crash. I built an interactive dashboard that shifts the two periods against each other and measures the correlation at every lag. The results show that most of the similarity comes from curve fitting rather than a repeating pattern.
