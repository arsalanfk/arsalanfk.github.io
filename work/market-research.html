Market briefings

These are research reports I write for the investment group I trade with. Each report focuses on one question and works through positioning, options activity, technical levels and macroeconomic data before presenting a view and the level at which that view would be wrong.

Special briefing · July 20, 2026 · 7 pages, 11 charts

Semis Under Fire: SOX chartbook

With oil at $90, the SOX index had fallen 20% from its record. My view was that the decline was driven by investors reducing positions rather than by weaker demand. Investors were still buying dips, dealer gamma was negative and the EUV equipment supply constraint remained in place. I concluded that prices were more attractive but that a bottom had not been confirmed, and recommended building positions gradually.

Single-asset read-through · August 9, 2026 · 7 pages

Gold: the reversal after the jobs report

This report explains how gold moved from a 28% drawdown to its best week in seven months. Most of the move came from one mechanism: gold was trading inversely to rate-hike expectations while the oil shock pushed inflation higher. Physical demand remained strong throughout, and the reversal call included a dated exit condition.

Cross-asset briefing · April 29, 2026 · 3 pages

Market outlook: from growth scare to supply-shock steepener

This briefing covers the shift from a bull flattening of the yield curve in the first quarter to a bear steepening after Middle East crude supply was disrupted, while equities reached new highs on earnings growth rather than higher valuations. It explains which parts of the curve mattered and why the 2s10s spread was not the right measure to watch.
